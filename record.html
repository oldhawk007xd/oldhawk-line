Check it yourself.

You do not have to take our word for any number on this page.

  1. Open a day's proof.It lists that day's commits on GitHub. The dates are stamped by GitHub, not by us.
  2. Compare the close.The last commit of the day holds the reading you see in the table.
  3. Take the data.history.json is the full record, free to use with credit to the Oldhawk Line.
  4. Method changes are dated.The method has a version number. Every change is a commit to the method's code, with its date, and never rewrites a past close.

Changes so far: on 28 and 30 Oct 2026 the rules for which coins count as runners were tightened (look-alike tokens, rigged markets and bundled launches are left out). The closes before each change stand as published. The method's full history on GitHub
